# 0.97.6

A clearer Usage tab, GPT-6 Sol and Luna back in the Codex picker, Muse thinking you can read, and Continuum Unified keeping pace with a turn running on your Mac.

## Models

- GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna stay selectable for Codex sessions. Codex CLI 0.156 dropped them from its model list, so starting a session on Sol failed; Continuum now keeps the models your account still offers.
- Meta Muse reasoning shows up as a Thinking row, on the Mac and on Linux and Windows hosts, just like every other provider.

## Usage

- Session origin is now a donut for your whole account plus a small pie per provider, so the split between Continuum-driven, External and Unknown usage reads at a glance. Hover or tap a slice for its amount and share. Available on Mac, iPhone, web and the desktop apps.
- New Settings page: Account & Sync > Continuum Inference Usage shows how much of your weekly hosted allowance you have used and what is left.
- Your hosted allowance is now usable all the way to your plan's weekly budget. Large requests were being refused with "weekly budget reached" while a few dollars of allowance remained.

## Billing

- Starting the Plus 7-day free trial from the pricing page now actually starts the trial instead of charging on day one.
- Trial days granted by support are applied to your subscription, so your plan stays active through the new date.

## Continuum Unified and Code

- A turn running in the native Mac app stays live in Continuum Unified: live timer, expanded activity and the follow-up composer, instead of looking finished.
- A new worktree with no messages opens on the centered "Build something great" canvas instead of "Conversation history is missing", and its tab is named after the repo.
- When the sidebar has no worktrees left, Code opens on the centered composer instead of an old conversation.
- The archive toast stays on the right edge of the window instead of stretching across it.
